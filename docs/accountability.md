# Accountability: who answers for a crossing

The principle: **every crossing below grade C gets a named responsible body, a dated formal
report with a protocol number, and a public counter of days without a fix.** A complaint without
a protocol number does not exist administratively; a counter without a named body blames no one.

Responsibility below is the general pattern. It varies street by street, so confirm it for each
crossing before naming anyone on the map.

## Who is responsible for what

| Question | Usually responsible | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns and maintains the street (paint, signs, ramps, footway)? | The **municipality** for local streets: Διεύθυνση Τεχνικών Υπηρεσιών (in Athens, Διεύθυνση Οδοποιίας, Αποχέτευσης και Κοινοχρήστων Χώρων). The **Region** for main arteries (in Attica, the Region's metropolitan infrastructure directorate). The Ministry of Infrastructure or a concessionaire for national roads. | Ask the municipality's technical service which body owns the street; municipal road-marking contracts on Διαύγεια show which streets a municipality treats as its own. |
| Who decides a new crossing, hump, one-way or school street? | The **municipal council**, by regulatory decision (κανονιστική απόφαση) following a traffic study by the technical service; decisions are published in the Government Gazette. | Council minutes, Διαύγεια. |
| Who is the political owner? | The **mayor** and the deputy mayors for technical works and for education; the president of the **Δημοτική Κοινότητα** for the neighbourhood. | Municipality website. |
| Who represents the school? | The **school head**, the **Σύλλογος Γονέων και Κηδεμόνων**, and the municipal education committee (Δημοτική Επιτροπή Παιδείας). | The school. |
| Who provides the crossing guard? | The school and municipality select and pay the volunteer σχολικός τροχονόμος. | School head. |
| Who enforces speed and parking? | **Τροχαία** (speed, moving offences) and **Δημοτική Αστυνομία** (illegal parking on crossings, ramps and corners). | Local traffic police department; municipal police. |
| Who maintains a "smart" crossing after its 2-year warranty? | Not settled in public sources. Ask the municipality in writing. | ΤΕΕ programme documents; the municipality. |

## The escalation ladder

Each step is logged on the crossing's record (`status`, `reported_on`, `protocol_no`).

1. **Audit and photograph.** Grade below C, or any critical failure.
2. **Formal request to the owner** using [the template](templates/aitisi-dimos.md), submitted
   through the municipality's protocol (in person, by email to the πρωτόκολλο, or through gov.gr
   where offered). Cite ΥΑ ΔΜΕΟ/ο/3050/2013 and the accessibility decision. Keep the protocol number.
   Co-sign with the parents' association and school head whenever possible: an institution's
   letter is harder to ignore than an individual's.
3. **Wait for the statutory reply period.** The Code of Administrative Procedure
   (ν. 2690/1999, άρθρο 4) obliges authorities to answer requests within a fixed period
   (50 days in the original text; **verify** the current figure). The map counter keeps running.
4. **Raise it in public.** Question at the Δημοτική Κοινότητα council and the municipal council;
   the opposition groups will usually table it.
5. **Συνήγορος του Πολίτη** (the Ombudsman, including its children's rights section) for
   maladministration or no reply. For accessibility failures, also the municipality's
   accessibility plan (ΣΑΠ) and the national disability confederation (ΕΣΑμεΑ).
6. **Press and the public league table.** See [promotion.md](promotion.md).

Record a fix the same way: date, photo, and a re-audit. Thank the service by name. Authorities
that fix things should look good on this map; that is half of what makes the pressure work.

## Following the money

- **Διαύγεια** (diavgeia.gov.gr): search each municipality for "διαγράμμιση", "διαβάσεις πεζών",
  "σήμανση", "ράμπες" to find contracts, budgets and contractors. A municipality that spends
  nothing on markings in a year has answered the maintenance question.
- **ΚΗΜΔΗΣ** for the tenders themselves, including contractor warranty periods: a crossing that
  fades within the warranty is the contractor's cost, not the taxpayer's.
- Typical scale from published tenders: municipal road-marking contracts range from a few tens
  of thousands of euros to about €200,000 a year. Repainting crossings is cheap; this is a
  question of priority, not money.

## What to ask every municipality once a year

1. How many school crossings do you have, and when was each last inspected and repainted?
2. Which schools have a crossing guard, and which do not?
3. What is your plan and budget line for applying ΥΑ ΔΜΕΟ/ο/3050/2013 around each school?
4. Who maintains the "smart" crossings after the warranty ends?
5. Which streets in front of schools will become school streets, and by when?

The answers, or the silence, go on the municipality's public page.

## Fairness rules

- Name **institutions and offices**, not individual employees. Elected officials are named in
  their public role only.
- Publish the authority's reply in full next to the complaint.
- Correct errors within a week and say so publicly.
- No photographs showing children's faces or readable number plates.

# Coalitions

Who to work with, what each brings and what to ask of them. None of these organisations has been
contacted; this is a target list. Check current contacts before approaching anyone.

## Core coalition (approach first)

| Partner | Brings | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Σύλλογοι Γονέων και Κηδεμόνων**, and the municipal **Ενώσεις Γονέων** | Legitimacy, volunteers at every school gate, signatures on requests | One ambassador per school; co-sign requests |
| **School heads and teachers' associations** | Access to the school community, crossing guards, classroom audits | Permission for noticeboards and pupil audits |
| **Ινστιτούτο Οδικής Ασφάλειας (Ι.Ο.ΑΣ.) «Πάνος Μυλωνάς»** | Greece's best-known road safety institute; has already delivered crossing improvements with the Municipality of Athens | Technical review of the method; joint launch |
| **SOS Τροχαία Εγκλήματα** and **ΕΥΘΥΤΑ** (road victims' organisations) | Moral authority; led the 2021 call of 159 organisations for 30 km/h | Co-sign; voice on the Day of Remembrance |
| **Disability organisations: ΕΣΑμεΑ** and local associations | Authority on accessibility; legal expertise | Review of the accessibility criteria; joint complaints on ramps |
| **Pedestrian rights groups**, e.g. Ένωση για τα Δικαιώματα των Πεζών | Long experience pressing municipalities on sidewalks | Share cases and tactics |

## How to build it

1. **Start with schools, not organisations.** Three parents' associations in one municipality are
   worth more than ten logos.
2. **One clear, small ask per partner**, as in the tables above.
3. **A short shared statement** partners can sign: every school crossing at C or better; a named
   responsible service for each; public maintenance records.
4. **Keep editorial independence.** Sponsors and municipalities can help and be thanked; they do
   not change grades.
5. **Offer something back**: open data, the method, and credit for every fix.
